AbstractWe present a twin-stick shooter in which the player is a cat in coherent superposition of up to sixteen positions (256, briefly, with help). Every bullet that touches the cat is a projective measurement, and survival follows the Born rule. Nine chapters suspend twenty-five laws of physics and end in nine bosses. We find no evidence that this is a good idea.

Field guide

Supplementary material. Everything on the screen, explained.

You are Schrödinger’s cat, and you can be in several places at once.

  1. Split to become 2 cats, then 4, 8 and 16. Every cat fires, so more cats means a lot more firepower. New cats can’t be hit for a moment while they fly out (they look faded).
  2. When a bullet or enemy touches any of your cats, the universe measures you. With N cats you die with probability 1/N. If you survive, only the cat that was touched disappears. With a single cat, a hit is certain death.
  3. Staying split drains coherence (the bar under your cats’ histogram). Enemies near you drain it faster. When it hits zero, you collapse into one cat at random. That is decoherence.
  4. Fold before that happens: every cat merges back into one and releases a shockwave that wipes out nearby bullets and hurts enemies. The more cats you fold, the bigger the blast, and you get some coherence back.
  5. A fold is also a dodge. You collapse onto the cat furthest in the direction you are steering, and you can’t be hit while it happens. Fold just as something is about to hit you for a perfect fold: a bigger blast and coherence back.
  6. Hold focus (Shift) to move slowly, pull your cats into a tight formation and show their hitboxes. Use it to thread gaps.
  7. Enemies aim at your expectation value ⟨x⟩, the average position of all your cats. When you are spread out, that average can be empty space.
  8. Every wave suspends one law of physics. The card at the start of the wave names it, and the line under the wave name at the top of the screen says what it does. Every fourth wave is a boss.

Elites

Between an enemy and a boss. Each has a health bar, a signature move, and always drops a power-up.

Pick-ups

Space weather

Random events in Endless and Boss rush. They come more often the further you get, and black holes can arrive mid-boss.

The campaign suspends one law per wave, then two or three at once in the last chapters. Endless mixes them at random. You can switch any combination on in the Laboratory.

Modes

Campaign. Nine chapters of four waves: three suspended laws, then a boss. Beat the ninth boss, The Apparatus, to open the box. You can start from any chapter you have reached.

Endless. Random combinations of laws, elites, space weather and a random boss every fifth wave. It never stops getting harder, and the music walks through every chapter’s track.

Boss rush. All nine bosses back to back, each with a random law suspended, then around again, tougher. The strip under the wave label shows how far you are.

Difficulty

Six levels: High School (for learning), Undergrad, Graduate, Postdoc, Reviewer 2 and Nobel Committee. Harder settings shorten every warning, narrow the gaps in bullet walls, and multiply your score and citations. Each difficulty remembers its own furthest chapter. Endless and Boss rush open when you finish the campaign on Undergrad or harder; Nobel Committee opens when you finish it on Reviewer 2.

Interpretations

After your first boss you choose how to read quantum mechanics. It lasts the whole run and decides which discoveries you are offered.

Medals and the cabinet

Medals come only from mastery: beating a boss without being measured, finishing the campaign on a harder difficulty, beating your best score by a clear margin. Spend them in the cabinet on cat designs, bullet trails and colour palettes.

Speedrun splits

Switch on speedrun splits in Settings for a timer per wave, compared with your personal best (green is ahead) and your best ever time for that wave (gold).

Discoveries (during a run)

Every boss you beat publishes a paper, and you choose one of three discoveries that last for the rest of the run: faster fire, homing photons, bigger folds, quantum immortality and more. The pause menu lists the ones you have.

Citations and grants (permanent)

Every run earns citations based on score, waves cleared and bosses beaten, scaled by difficulty. The first time you beat each boss pays extra, and so does every publication (achievement). Spend citations in the grant office on permanent upgrades. You can switch grants off for a pure run, or refund them at any time.

Saving

Records, statistics, citations and settings are saved in this browser automatically, so they are still there next time you open the game here. To move them to another device or browser, use Export save in Settings and paste the code into Import save there.
